# Research progress and discussion with Hillary

Jason Duran Dutton | Portfolio update and meeting brief | 23 September 2026

## Purpose of our discussion

I would like to discuss how my current independent research can be evaluated and developed into a credible next stage of work. My long-term scientific goal is to derive physical matter from a mathematically consistent field framework, motivated by a primordial unity hypothesis, and to investigate whether the resulting excitations can produce a geometry universally experienced by matter.

This remains an ambitious research objective. I am not presenting it as a completed theory or an experimentally established result. The immediate value of the latest work is a clearer mathematical model, executable numerical tests, and a more precise account of what succeeds and what fails.

## What has changed in the research

The investigation has moved from a broad conceptual framework to specific equations and tests. A simple field model supports an effective geometry for one type of wave. Numerical calculations then construct a moving vortex ring. An explicitly proposed second field allows charge to be concentrated around the ring, and the two fields are solved together.

The latest limited disturbance tests show that this moving charged loop persists over the tested interval. The calculations include resolution checks, conserved-charge monitoring, an unperturbed control and a distinction between phase drift and shape change. These are useful computational checks, but they do not establish an observed particle or complete stability.

Just as importantly, a mathematical scaling argument rules out one desired rest-state interpretation for the original parameter choice. A revised parameter range avoids that particular obstruction, but the first searches did not establish a new rest-loop solution. This negative result helps define the next research question rather than being concealed by the numerical successes.

## How this fits the existing portfolio

SEFI, DEFI and GWFM provide the research history and motivation. In the present investigation, earlier manuscripts are not treated as established laws of nature. Proposed connections are accepted only when the equations support them and they are consistent with established physics.

The software contribution is an independent, opt-in research addition to the SEFI-PY Evolution workspace. It records the model, equations, numerical profiles, checks and unsuccessful searches while preserving the existing engine. It demonstrates a reproducible computational workflow; it does not validate every physical interpretation associated with the wider portfolio.
